## Rules for using this registry

1. **Scope symbols to lecture and model**, not globally to course.
2. When the original lecture is accessible, open the original page/equation before making an exact notation claim.
3. Do not substitute notation from a different lecture or textbook without explicitly naming the mapping.
4. Do not treat INDEXED as REVIEWED: record exact verified equation/page ranges.
5. Mark provisional meanings when only extracted text or a partial snippet was inspected.
