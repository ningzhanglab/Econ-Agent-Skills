## Motivation

The user compared three decks on Bai (2025), "Melons as Lemons" (*Review of Economic Studies* 92): one made by ChatGPT with no skill, one by ChatGPT with `econ-present` (a 60-minute talk), and one made by Claude and then revised by hand. A single reviewer scored them from the PDFs, out of 12: no skill 8, with the skill 9, Claude and human-revised 10. Every number checked matched the paper in all three decks. The reviewer's notes, as reported by the user:

- The skill deck had cleaner titles, Appendix links both ways and no source-line clutter, but covered less than the no-skill deck, had no bridge slides, omitted N from its tables, and showed only panel (a) of Figure 5 with a stray "(b)" label.
- Most of these are rules `SKILL.md` already states: bridge slides at section transitions (Plan the story first, step 3), every section somewhere (step 5), N and units in tables (Each slide, Tables). The deck missed rules that exist; it did not lack rules.
- Figure cropping went wrong in both real-paper runs. On Jones (2026) the crops made in TeX embedded the whole source page beneath the clip ([2026-11-16 log](2026-11-16-econ-present-real-paper-comparison.md)); on Melons a multi-panel figure was cut. `SKILL.md` said to crop from the page but gave no way to do it.

The user asked to keep the core rules and add only what the skill truly lacks. Following the design's rule that a skill that does not beat no skill should lose rules rather than gain them, the change adds one capability and moves text that only some requests need out of the page read on every request.

## Changes made

- **Added `scripts/crop_figure.py`.** It renders one PDF page, or a box on it given as fractions of the page, to a PNG, using PyMuPDF or else Poppler's `pdftoppm` and `pdfinfo`. Without either it fails with a message to crop by hand and say so in the handover. The image holds only the cropped area. It ends by asking the agent to open the image and check that every panel is whole.
- **`SKILL.md`, Figures:** one clause names the script, says not to trim the page in TeX, and says to open the saved image to see that every panel is whole and nothing else from the page is left in.
- **Moved out of `SKILL.md`, wording unchanged:**
  - "Revising an existing deck" became `tasks/revise-deck.md`.
  - The content-file paragraph became `tasks/content-file.md`.
  - `SKILL.md` gains a two-row "Other requests" table that routes to them. The Defaults line saying a content file is made only on request stays in `SKILL.md`.
- **`SKILL.md`, Titles:** the paragraph is shortened. All its requirements stay ("Topic: point", one line, the same short topic across a section, plain Introduction, Outline and Conclusion, bold lead-in for a Proposition, an Appendix title with a subtitle); one example and two sentences of rationale were dropped.
- **Not added:** the reviewer's other suggestions (more lint checks, a handover template, a presentation rubric, extra wording on bridge slides, N and units). Each either repeats a rule that exists or answers no failure that was observed.

## Behavior and compatibility

- A new-deck request reads a slightly shorter `SKILL.md`: about 2,800 words instead of about 3,090, a cut of under 10 percent. That is less than the "about a third" estimated before the change.
- Revise and content-file requests now open one more page.
- Figures are cropped to an image instead of trimmed in TeX.
- No rule was removed or changed in meaning.

## Known limitations and risks

- The Melons scores come from one reviewer reading PDFs, one run per deck, with no blinding; the skill-made deck was made by a different model and for a fixed 60-minute length, which may explain part of its thinner coverage. The comparison was not rerun here, and the reviewer's slide-level findings were not rechecked against the PDFs because no PDF renderer was available.
- Whether a shorter `SKILL.md` improves story and coverage is a hypothesis, not a result.
- The cropper takes the box as fractions of the page, so the agent must estimate it from a page image. A wrong box is caught only by looking at the saved image.
